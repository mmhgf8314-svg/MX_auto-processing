# Who is who

Roles, not a contact list — addresses live in `config/routing.toml`, and the
authoritative version of any thread's participants is the thread itself.

## Matrox side (English)

| Role | What they need from you |
|---|---|
| Inside sales / order desk | PO number, part number, quantity, requested date, ship-from preference, payment status |
| Regional / BD management | Deal context, customer name, what is at stake, what decision you need |
| Product & OEM support | Product, firmware/driver version, host hardware, OS build, configuration, reproduction steps, case number |
| Legal / contracts | Which agreement, which counterparty, what changed |
| Marketing (`video@matrox.com`) | Nothing — bulk mail, skipped by triage |

## Japan side (Japanese unless the thread says otherwise)

| Role | What they need from you |
|---|---|
| Distributor — sales & logistics | Delivery dates, stock, quotations, shipment and tracking. Their logistics staff often write to head office in English directly; match whatever language they used. |
| Distributor — engineering | Technical answers in Japanese, with product names left in Latin script |
| Field technical partner | Bilingual. Often the bridge to head office engineering — check whether they have already escalated before you draft a duplicate report. |
| End customers (broadcasters, integrators, developers) | Japanese, formal keigo. Never expose Matrox-internal case numbers or internal discussion. |

## Traps

- The owner's private mail also lands in this mailbox. Non-Matrox personal
  domains are in the ignore list and must stay there.
- Some head-office mail is addressed to `taku_yamashita@matrox.com` and
  forwarded. It is still the Matrox side.
- A customer thread can be conducted entirely in English. Check the thread,
  not the domain, before choosing a language.
- Head office and the distributor sometimes correspond directly with the owner
  only in cc. Being in cc is not a reason to draft — draft when an answer is
  owed by the owner.
